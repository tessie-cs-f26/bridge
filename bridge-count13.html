Count to 13

Three suit lengths appear. How many cards are in the fourth suit? Answer fast for more points.

How it works
  • A bridge hand has 13 cards. Three of its suit lengths appear, one after another, and you supply the fourth (0 to 7).
  • Click a number or type it. A right answer within half a second of the third number scores 10 points; each extra tenth of a second costs 1 point, down to 1. A wrong answer scores 0.
  • 10 problems per round, so the best possible score is 100.
