Opening Bids

You’re the dealer. Look at the vulnerability and your hand, then pick your opening bid, 2/1 style.

How to choose
  • Count points = high card points (A 4, K 3, Q 2, J 1) + length points (+1 for each card past four in a suit).
  • 11 or fewer: Pass. 22 or more: 2♣︎.
  • Balanced (4-3-3-3, 4-4-3-2, 5-3-3-2, 5-card minor with 4-2-2, or 6-card minor with 3-2-2): 15 to 17 opens 1NT, and 20 to 21 opens 2NT.
  • Otherwise open 1 of your longest suit, but never a 4-card major. With two 5-card (or 6-card) suits, open the higher-ranking one. With no 5-card suit, open a minor: 4-4 in the minors opens 1♦︎, 3-3 opens 1♣︎, otherwise the longer minor.
  • Exception: with 6+ cards in a minor, a 5-card major, and fewer than 16 points, open 1 of the major.
  • Click a level and a suit (or NT) in either order, or Pass. Keyboard: 1–7, then S, H, D, C, or N; P to pass.
