Opening Leads

The opponents are in notrump or a suit, and you’ve decided to lead this suit. Which card do you lead?

Lead conventions
  • Doubleton: the top card. A-K: the A.
  • Sequences: the top of a sequence (KQJ, QJ10, J109) or a broken sequence (KQ10, QJ9), and the top of an interior sequence (the J from KJ10, the 10 from K109 or A109, the 9 from J98). Against a suit contract, also the top of two touching honors (the K from KQ7).
  • Against notrump: lead high only from a sequence or near-sequence of three cards (KQJ, KQ10, QJ9, J108, 1097). Otherwise lead 4th best from a holding with a high card (A, K, Q or J), or low from three to an honor. From KQ73, lead the 3.
  • Against a suit, “3rd and low” setting: 3rd best from an even number of cards, lowest from an odd number (the 2 from 8-6-2), with or without an honor.
  • Against a suit, “4th best” setting: 4th best from four or more cards, and the lowest from three.
  • Notrump, no high card: 2nd from weakness (the 7 from 9-7-5-3), and 4th best is also accepted. With only three cards, lead the top of nothing (the 9 from 9-7-3), but the 2nd card from 10-x-x. From 10-9-x-x, lead the 10.
  • Against a suit contract you won’t see the A without the K, since you’d rarely lead away from an ace.
  • Click a card, or type its rank (A, K, Q, J, T or 0 for the 10, 9 to 2).
