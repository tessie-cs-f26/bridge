Responding to 1NT

Partner opens 1NT (15 to 17). Pick your response, using basic Stayman, Jacoby and Texas transfers, and minor-suit transfers.

How to respond
  • Count points = high card points + length points (+1 for each card past four in a suit). With a 6-card or longer major, you’re sure of a fit and will be the long trump hand, so count shortness instead: doubleton +1, singleton +2, void +3.
  • Stayman: with a 4-card major and 8 or more points, bid 2♣︎. Exception: with flat 4-3-3-3 shape, ignore the 4-card major and raise notrump.
  • Texas transfers: with a 6-card or longer major and 10–15 points (game, but no slam interest), bid 4♦︎ for hearts or 4♥︎ for spades. With 6-4 in the majors, bid Stayman first; you can Texas on the next round if no 4-4 fit turns up.
  • Jacoby transfers: with a 5-card or longer major (and no 4-card major to look for), bid the suit below it: 2♦︎ for hearts, 2♥︎ for spades. With 5-5 in the majors: weak (under 8 HCP), transfer to hearts and bid 2♠︎ next; game forcing (8+ HCP), transfer to spades and bid 3♥︎ next. With 6-5, do the same, treating the six as five (game forcing takes only 7+ HCP), and insist on the 6-card suit if partner tries to sign off in 3NT. Transfer with any strength.
  • Minor-suit transfers: with a 6-card or longer minor in an unbalanced hand (a singleton, a void, or a 7-card suit), bid 2♠︎ to transfer to clubs or 3♣︎ to transfer to diamonds. With 6-3-2-2, treat the hand as balanced and use the notrump raises.
  • No major to show: 7 or fewer, pass. 8–9: 2NT. 10–15: 3NT. 16–17: 4NT. 18–19: 6NT. 20–21: 5NT. 22+: 7NT.
  • Click a level and a strain (or Pass). Keyboard: 1–7, then S, H, D, C or N; P to pass.
  • Each round is a race of 10 hands: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
