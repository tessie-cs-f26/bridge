Responding to 1 of a Suit

Partner opens one of a suit. Pick your response, playing 2/1 with a semi-forcing 1NT.

How to respond
  • Points = HCP + length points. With 3+ card support for partner’s major, count support points instead: HCP + 1 for a doubleton, 3 for a singleton, 5 for a void.
  • 1♠︎ with fewer than 3 spades: 0–5 with no ace, pass; an ace or 6–12, 1NT (semi-forcing); 13+, a game-forcing 2/1: 2♥︎ or 2♦︎ with 5+ cards, otherwise 2♣︎.
  • 1♠︎ with 3–4 spades (support points): 0–2 pass; 3–5 1NT, then 2♠︎; 6–10 2♠︎; 11–12 with 3 spades 1NT, then jump in spades; 11–12 with 4 spades 3♠︎; 13+ with 4 spades 2NT (Jacoby), or with a singleton or void a splinter (a double jump in that suit: 4♣︎, 4♦︎ or 4♥︎); 13+ with 3 spades, a 2/1, then support spades.
  • Game raise: with 5+ trumps, 5–9 HCP and a singleton or void, jump straight to 4 of the major. Without shortness, treat 5 trumps like 4.
  • 1♥︎: the same, using heart support (splinters are 3♠︎, 4♣︎ and 4♦︎), but bid 1♠︎ instead of 1NT with 4 spades. With 13+ points, 4 spades and fewer than 4 hearts, prefer a 2/1 to set up a game force.
  • 1♣︎ or 1♦︎: respond with any ace or 6+ points. Bid your longer 4+ card major (1♥︎ with 4-4, 1♠︎ with 5-5 or 6-6).
  • Over 1♣︎ with 5+ diamonds: bid 1♦︎, unless you also hold a 4-card major and fewer than 13 points (then bid the major).
  • 5-card support for the minor (no 4-card major): 5–9 HCP jump to 3 of the minor; 10+ HCP raise to 2; 13+ HCP with a singleton or void in a major, splinter in that major at the 3-level. Raising beats notrump.
  • Over 1♦︎ with 5+ clubs: 13+ bid 2♣︎ (even with a 4-card major, to set up the game force); 10–12 with 6 clubs jump to 3♣︎ (invitational jump shift), with 5 clubs raise to 2♦︎; 6–9 with (31)45 shape, lie with 1NT.
  • Otherwise, balanced with no 4-card major: 6–10 1NT, 11–12 2NT, 13–15 3NT.
  • Invitational jump shift over a major: with fewer than 3-card support, 10–12 points and a 6-card suit ranking below partner’s, jump in it (1♠︎–3♣︎, 1♥︎–3♦︎).
  • Must respond (Marty Rosenberg’s rules), even when weak: with an ace; with a 5-card major you can bid at the 1-level (bid it); with 6 hearts over 1♠︎ (bid 1NT); with a void after a first-seat 1♥︎ or 1♠︎; with 4-card support for partner’s major; with 3-card support for partner’s major when not vulnerable (1NT, or 1♠︎ over 1♥︎ with 4 spades); over 1♦︎ vulnerable with a singleton or void diamond; over 1♣︎ with a singleton or void club; over 1♣︎ vulnerable with a doubleton club.
  • Each round is a race of 10 hands: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
