How to read the questions
  • As declarer, you are South and dummy is North. “On your left” means West, the player who plays before dummy when you lead from your hand.
  • An “x” is any small card that can’t win a trick.
  • Each round goes through every question in the topic you picked, once each, at your own pace. Each right answer is 1 point, and you can post your score to the class leaderboard at the end.
  • Defense questions show your seat and the cards you can see.
  • Goal: unless the question names a target (like “you need four tricks”), the answer is the play that wins the most tricks on average, using the odds alone. Real deals add clues from the bidding and the play that can change the best line.
  • Carding: attitude signals follow the Attitude signals setting. With standard signals a high card encourages; with upside-down signals a low card encourages. Count is always standard: high-low shows an even number of cards. Opening leads are standard: the A from AK, the top of a sequence, fourth best otherwise.
