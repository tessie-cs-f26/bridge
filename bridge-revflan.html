Reverse Flannery

After 1♣︎ or 1♦︎, responder shows 5+ spades and 4+ hearts. Drill the responses, the 3♣︎ continuation, and what opener’s rebids mean.

The system
  • Points = HCP + length points (1 for each card past four in a suit).
  • 0–3 points: pass, even with both majors.
  • 12+ points, or 9+ points with 5+ hearts: respond 1♠︎ (too strong, or too shapely, for reverse Flannery).
  • Weak (4–8 points, 5+ spades, 4+ hearts): 1♣︎–2♦︎, or 1♦︎–2♥︎.
  • Invitational (9–11 points, 5–6 spades, exactly 4 hearts): over 1♣︎, 2♥︎ with 5 spades, 2♠︎ with 6; over 1♦︎, 2♠︎ with either.
  • After opener’s 3♣︎ (asks you to describe): weak: 3 of a major with extra length; otherwise 3♦︎ with 4–6, 3NT with 6–8. Invitational: 3♥︎ or 3♠︎ with a good suit (2 of the top 3 or 3 of the top 5 honors); otherwise 3♦︎ with 9, or 10 with 3+ queens and jacks; 3NT with 10–11.
  • Opener’s rebids: 2♥︎, 2♠︎ and 3NT are to play; 3♥︎ and 3♠︎ are natural game invitations, except that after a 2♠︎ response 3♥︎ is to play (no room for both, and playing in hearts takes priority); 3♣︎ asks responder to describe; 3♦︎ is game forcing with 6+ cards in the minor opened; 2NT is a signoff in the minor or a slam try in a major.
  • Each round is a race of 10 questions: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
